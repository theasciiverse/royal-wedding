The permission covers his games in Asciiverse. The terminal runtime written
for it (`asciiverse/rotty/`, `asciiverse/build.py`, `asciiverse/game.js`
and the build and packaging files) is MIT, the same as Asciiverse's `av`;

`asciiverse/quickjs/LICENSE`. rot.js, bundled by the game as
